# Mini-Review Protocol — RAG for Email (Pilot Run)
Date: 2026-10-06
Reviewers: Saad Namoune, Chourouk Chaker

## Research question
Which published works address retrieval-augmented generation 
applied specifically to email corpora?

## Databases
- arXiv (cs.CL + cs.IR)
- ACL Anthology

## Search query
"retrieval augmented generation" AND "email"

## Inclusion criteria
- Published 2020 or later
- Addresses RAG or retrieval+LLM applied to email
- Written in English
- Accessible as full text

## Exclusion criteria  
- Email classification or spam without retrieval
- Pre-2020 retrieval systems
- Position papers under 4 pages

## Screening procedure
Both reviewers screen titles and abstracts independently in Rayyan.
Disagreements resolved by discussion.
Report Cohen's kappa.
